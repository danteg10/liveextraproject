On the day

More than a round of golf.

There's a way to be part of this whether or not you pick up a club.

Silent Auction

A silent auction will be held throughout the day with a variety of prizes. Donations of auction items are always welcome — reach out to Nick if you'd like to contribute.

Put your name, your family's name, or your business on a hole. It's one of the simplest ways to support the outing and be remembered on the course.

All proceeds from the outing benefit sarcoma cancer research — the same fight Dominic gave everything to, carried forward by everyone who shows up.
